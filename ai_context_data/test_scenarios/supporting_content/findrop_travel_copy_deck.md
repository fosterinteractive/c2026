## Feature Benefits (6 items)

1. **Book in minutes, not meetings** — Flights, hotels, trains, and rental cars in one app.
2. **Policy is built in** — Company travel policy shows up as you book. Green means go.
3. **Zero expense reports** — Travel expenses reconcile automatically. No receipts to photograph.
4. **Save the company money, keep a reward** — Book under budget and earn a share of the savings.
5. **Keep your loyalty points** — Airline miles and hotel points are yours.
6. **24/7 support when plans change** — Real support from people who can rebook you.

## Finance Team Section (3 cards for secondary audience)

1. **Live travel spend visibility** — See every booking the moment it's made. Track travel budgets by department, project, or individual.

3. **Automatic reconciliation** — Travel expenses flow directly into your accounting software with the right GL codes and cost centers.

## FAQ (6 items)

**Q: What travel inventory does FinDrop Travel offer?**
A: Access to all major airlines, 2M+ hotel properties, rental car agencies, and rail options worldwide.

**Q: How is policy enforced?**
A: Policy rules are applied live as employees search and book. Out-of-policy selections trigger an approval workflow before confirmation.

**Q: Do travelers keep their loyalty program status and points?**
A: Yes. FinDrop Travel integrates with major airline and hotel loyalty programs.

**Q: How does FinDrop Travel connect to FinDrop Cards and Expense?**
A: They're one platform. Travel bookings automatically flow into the same dashboard and accounting sync as card transactions and expenses.

**Q: What about complex international trips or VIP executive travel?**
A: FinDrop Travel handles the 80% of trips that are straightforward. For complex multi-city international itineraries, we integrate with premium TMC partners.

**Q: How long does implementation take?**
A: Most companies are live within 10 business days.
