2. **90%+ adoption rates** — Employees actually use it
3. **Implementation in days** — Live within 10 business days
4. **Automatic reconciliation** — No manual expense reports
5. **Enterprise-grade security** — SOC 2 Type II, bank-level encryption, real-time fraud monitoring

### Target Audiences

- Travel Program Managers
- CFOs and Controllers
- Finance Teams
- **IT/Security Teams** (new in v7)

### Competitive Positioning

- Easier to use than legacy T&E solutions
- More integrated than point solutions

- **Enterprise security that outperforms [Competitor X] by 40%** (new in v7)

## New in Version 7

### Enterprise Security Differentiator

**Headline:** Enterprise Security Your IT Team Will Love

**Key Points:**
- SOC 2 Type II certified
- Bank-level 256-bit encryption
- Real-time fraud monitoring and alerting
- Role-based access controls
- SSO/SAML integration
- Audit logs for compliance

**Proof Point:** "Outperforms [Competitor X] by 40% on enterprise security benchmarks" (validated by third-party assessment)

**Why This Matters:**
- IT teams are increasingly involved in T&E vendor selection
- Security is becoming a top-3 evaluation criterion
- Competitors are weak on enterprise security messaging

## Governance Note

The competitive claim "Outperforms [Competitor X] by 40%" requires legal approval before use in published marketing materials per Brand Guidelines.

Approved alternatives:
- "Industry-leading security performance, validated by third-party benchmarks"
- "Enterprise-grade security that exceeds industry standards"
