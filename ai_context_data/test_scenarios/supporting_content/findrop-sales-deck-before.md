# FinDrop Sales Pitch Deck - Version 6 (Before)

This represents the sales deck BEFORE the sales team added the enterprise security differentiator.

Used in Test Case 4.1 to show what was missing.

---

## Key Messages (v6)

### Value Propositions

1. **All-in-one platform** — Cards, expenses, and travel in one place
2. **90%+ adoption rates** — Employees actually use it
3. **Implementation in days** — Live within 10 business days
4. **Automatic reconciliation** — No manual expense reports

### Target Audiences

- Travel Program Managers
- CFOs and Controllers
- Finance Teams

### Competitive Positioning

- Easier to use than legacy T&E solutions
- More integrated than point solutions
- Better adoption than competitors

## What's Missing (Added in v7)

- Third-party benchmark validation
