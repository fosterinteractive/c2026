## Common questions

### How long does implementation take?
Most companies are fully operational in 10 business days. We handle the integration with your accounting software, import your chart of accounts, and train your team. You can run FinDrop alongside your existing process during transition.

### What accounting software do you integrate with?
FinDrop syncs with 50+ platforms including QuickBooks, QuickBooks Online, and NetSuite. We also offer a REST API for custom integrations.

### Do employees have to do anything differently?
Less, actually. They use their FinDrop card normally. Receipts are captured automatically. There are no expense reports to file. If they pay out of pocket, they snap a photo and submit through the app — approval and reimbursement are faster with automated workflows.

### What happens with out-of-policy expenses?
FinDrop catches them in two ways. First, card-level controls can block out-of-policy merchants or amounts before purchase. Second, our policy engine flags any transaction that doesn't match your rules and routes it to the right approver automatically.

### Is it secure?
SOC 2 Type II certified. Bank-level 256-bit encryption. Every virtual card has a unique number. Role-based access controls. Full audit trail for every transaction and approval. More secure than a shared physical card in a desk drawer.
